How long does a typical project take?

Prototypes in 2–4 weeks; production rollouts typically 6–12 weeks depending on integrations.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We support private cloud and on-prem deployments, encryption, and never train public models on your data.

Do we need lots of data to start?

Not always. Modern LLMs and RAG let us deliver value with limited data, and we advise on collection when needed.

What does it cost?

Fixed-scope pilots start at a few thousand dollars; we share a clear estimate after a free discovery call.
